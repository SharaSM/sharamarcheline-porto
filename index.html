07What drives me

Helping ASEAN make a safe transition to AI.

I believe the world’s move to AI has to be safe, and it has to benefit everyone. I’ve seen what that looks like in practice. At Microsoft, we work on AI for inclusion, from AI for people with disabilities to partnerships with educational institutions. That work convinced me that AI, developed responsibly, can help drive human development for years to come.

ASEAN is where I want to help make that real. The region is a hub, and it is still growing fast. I’ve spent my career helping banks, telcos and government bodies adopt new technology, from Indonesia to Thailand, Malaysia and the Philippines. Now I want to help the region make a safe transition to AI-driven transformation: one that fuels economic growth and, just as importantly, gives people easier access to information and education.
